Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

At Anik Tools ("we", "our", or "us"), your privacy is a core value — not an afterthought. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect (and don't collect), how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide

Anik Tools does not require account registration. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily contact us via email or our contact form.

Download data

When you paste a video URL and download it, the following happens:

  • The URL is sent to our server to process the download.
  • The downloaded file is temporarily stored on the server and is automatically deleted within 30 minutes of transfer completion.
  • We do not log the URLs you download, and we do not build any profile of your activity.

Automatically collected information

Like most web applications, we may collect limited technical data for security and performance purposes, including IP address (for rate limiting), browser type, operating system, referrer URL, and request timestamps. This data is used solely for security and abuse prevention.

2. Cookies and Local Storage

Anik Tools uses minimal browser storage to enhance your experience:

  • Local storage — to remember your recent download history. This data stays entirely on your device and is never sent to us.
  • No tracking cookies — we do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies.

3. Third-Party Services

Anik Tools uses the following third-party services:

  • yt-dlp — a third-party open-source library used to retrieve videos from supported platforms.
  • Google AdSense — may display non-intrusive advertisements. See Google's Privacy Policy.

4. How We Use Information

The limited information we collect is used exclusively to provide and maintain the download service, prevent abuse and spam, improve service performance, and respond to your inquiries.

5. Data Retention

Downloaded files are automatically deleted from our server within 30 minutes. We do not archive or retain any download history. Server logs (if any) are retained for a maximum of 30 days.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect data, including HTTPS encryption for all connections, regular security updates, automatic file cleanup, and rate limiting to prevent abuse.

7. Children's Privacy

Anik Tools is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including GDPR and CCPA), you may have the right to access, delete, or opt out of data processing. Since we do not collect personal data or require accounts, most rights are automatically satisfied. Contact us at unitedstatemobilevpn@gmail.com to exercise any right.

9. International Users

Anik Tools is operated globally. By using the service, you consent to the processing of your information as described in this policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes take effect immediately upon posting. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at unitedstatemobilevpn@gmail.com.
