DMCA Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Anik Tools respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects its users to do the same. In accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA), we will respond promptly to valid notices of alleged copyright infringement.

Our position

Anik Tools is a general-purpose tool that allows users to download publicly available videos from various platforms. We do not host any video content on our servers. Downloaded files are stored temporarily on the user's own device or server and are automatically deleted within 30 minutes after transfer.

Because we do not host or display copyrighted content, DMCA notices typically apply to us only when a specific URL is being used through our Service to download infringing material.

Filing a DMCA Notice

If you believe your copyrighted work is being infringed through our Service, please send a written notice to our designated agent at:

Email: unitedstatemobilevpn@gmail.com

Your notice must include the following information:

  • Identification of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed — provide the title, author, and, if applicable, the URL of the original work.
  • Identification of the infringing material — provide the exact URL(s) you believe are being used to download the work through our Service.
  • Your contact information — full name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address.
  • A good faith statement — a statement that you have a good faith belief that the disputed use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  • Accuracy statement — a statement made under penalty of perjury that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorized to act on their behalf.
  • Physical or electronic signature — your full legal name as the signature.

What happens next

Upon receiving a valid DMCA notice, we will:

  • Review the notice within 1-3 business days.
  • If valid, take appropriate action — which may include blocking the specific URL from being processed through our Service.
  • Notify the affected party if applicable.

Counter-Notification

If you believe your content was removed in error, you may file a counter-notification with the same information format described above, clearly stating your reasons.

Repeat infringers

We do not maintain user accounts and therefore do not have a "repeat infringer" policy in the traditional sense. We will, however, block URLs that are the subject of valid DMCA notices.

Misrepresentation

Please note that under Section 512(f) of the DMCA, any person who knowingly materially misrepresents that material is infringing may be subject to liability for damages.

Contact

For DMCA-related questions or notices, contact us at unitedstatemobilevpn@gmail.com.
