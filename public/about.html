About Anik Tools

A free, modern, privacy-first video downloader built for people who want to save videos from the web without the noise, ads, or tracking that plague other tools.

Our mission

The internet is full of incredible content, but it's locked behind platforms that want to keep you online. Anik Tools exists to give you back control — so you can save the videos that matter to you, watch them wherever you want, and share them however you choose.

We believe a downloader should be:

  • Fast — downloading at the speed your connection allows, not slower.
  • Simple — no accounts, no pop-ups, no nonsense.
  • Private — we don't track what you save, and we never will.
  • Free — forever, with no hidden fees.

What we built

Anik Tools is a progressive web application (PWA) that works on any device with a browser. Under the hood, it uses a combination of modern web technologies and the powerful open-source tool yt-dlp to fetch and save videos from more than 1800 supported platforms, including:

  • YouTube, YouTube Music, YouTube Shorts
  • TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, Threads
  • X (formerly Twitter), Reddit, Tumblr
  • Vimeo, Dailymotion, Twitch, SoundCloud
  • And many more — with new platforms added regularly

The technology

We take security and speed seriously. Anik Tools is built on:

  • Node.js + Express for a fast, lightweight backend.
  • yt-dlp — the industry-standard open-source downloader, actively maintained by the community.
  • FFmpeg for high-quality audio and video processing.
  • Modern CSS and JavaScript for a smooth, responsive interface.

Privacy by design

We don't require an account. We don't store your downloads. We don't sell your data. Every download you make happens between you and the source — Anik Tools simply brokers the connection, then gets out of the way.

Read our Privacy Policy to learn more.

Who we are

Anik Tools is designed and built by Mahedi Hassan Anik, an independent developer who wanted a cleaner, faster alternative to the ad-ridden downloaders that litter the web. What started as a personal project is now used by thousands of people every day.
