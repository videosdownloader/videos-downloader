How to Use Anik Tools

Downloading any video takes just four simple steps. Here's how to get started.

Step 1: Copy the video link

Open the app or website where the video lives (YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, etc.) and copy the video's URL. On mobile, tap the Share button and choose Copy Link.

The URL usually looks like this:

  • https://youtube.com/watch?v=...
  • https://www.tiktok.com/@user/video/...
  • https://www.instagram.com/reel/...

Step 2: Paste the link

Return to Anik Tools and paste the URL into the input field at the top of the page. You can:

  • Tap the paste icon if you see it (it appears when a link is on your clipboard)
  • Or long-press the input and choose Paste
  • Or type or paste the URL manually

Anik Tools will automatically detect the source platform and show its icon next to the input.

Step 3: Fetch the video

Tap the Fetch Video button. Within a few seconds, we scan the source and display:

  • The video's thumbnail and title
  • The uploader name
  • The video's duration
  • All available quality options — from 360p up to 4K

Step 4: Choose format and quality, then download

Select your format:

  • Video (MP4) — full video with audio
  • Audio (MP3) — extract just the soundtrack

If you chose MP4, pick the resolution you want. We recommend the highest available for the best quality, or 720p for a good balance of quality and file size.

Tap Download Now. You'll see real-time progress with speed, ETA, and file size. When complete, the file will be saved to your device's Downloads folder.

Tips for the best experience

  • Use Wi-Fi for large files — 4K downloads can be 500MB+ each.
  • Check your storage — make sure you have enough free space.
  • Try a lower quality if the download stalls — sometimes a network issue is specific to a high bitrate stream.
  • Install as an app — add Anik Tools to your home screen for one-tap access.

Keyboard shortcuts (desktop)

  • Ctrl + K — Open command palette
  • / — Focus the input field
  • Enter — Fetch video
  • D — Start download
  • Esc — Clear input / close dialogs

Troubleshooting

The download failed

Try these steps in order:

  1. Verify the link opens in a browser — the video may have been removed or made private.
  2. Try a lower quality — high-resolution streams sometimes time out on slow connections.
  3. Refresh the page and try again.

Only some platforms work

Anik Tools supports 1800+ sites, but individual platforms occasionally change their systems. If a specific site stops working, please let us know — we update our engine regularly.

The video needs a login

Age-restricted, private, or member-only videos cannot be downloaded without a logged-in account. Anik Tools does not support authentication for privacy and security reasons.

Legal reminder

Only download content you own or have permission to use. Respect the copyright and terms of service of each platform.
